A few helpful details

Before you order.

How much notice do you need?

We recommend at least seven days for signature cakes and two to three weeks for larger event orders. A 15–25% rush fee may apply to orders accepted with fewer than 72 hours' notice.

Can you copy a cake from a photo?

Photos help us understand your preferred mood, colours, and details. We create an original Sweet Moment design inspired by your references rather than making an exact copy.

What is included in the starting price?

Each base price includes the stated size, one simple colour palette, and a short hand-lettered message. Specialty fillings, complex themes, toppers, HST, and delivery are additional.

Do you accommodate allergies?

Please share all dietary needs during consultation. Available options and cross-contact limitations will be discussed clearly before an order is accepted.
