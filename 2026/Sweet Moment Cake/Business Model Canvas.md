# Sweet Moment Cake

> Fresh, handmade cakes that turn birthdays, celebrations, and thoughtful gifts into sweet moments through personalized designs and convenient local delivery.

## Business Model Canvas

### 1. Customer Segments

- Families celebrating birthdays and milestones
- Couples celebrating anniversaries or engagements
- Parents ordering cakes for children
- Offices and small businesses
- Customers seeking personalized gifts
- Local event planners and party organizers

### 2. Value Propositions

- Fresh, handmade cakes prepared in small batches
- Custom designs, flavors, messages, and sizes
- Convenient and reliable local delivery
- Attractive packaging suitable for gifting
- Personal service from ordering through delivery
- Dietary options where practical
- Memorable cakes made for meaningful occasions

### 3. Channels

- Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook
- Business website or online ordering page
- WhatsApp, phone, and direct messages
- Google Business Profile
- Local markets and community events
- Customer referrals
- Partnerships with cafes, florists, and party planners

### 4. Customer Relationships

- Friendly, personalized order consultations
- Order and delivery confirmation messages
- Design approval for custom cakes
- Follow-up requests for reviews and feedback
- Loyalty rewards for repeat customers
- Birthday and anniversary reminders
- Referral discounts

### 5. Revenue Streams

- Standard cake sales
- Custom and premium cake orders
- Delivery fees based on distance
- Cupcakes, mini cakes, and dessert boxes
- Cake-and-gift packages
- Corporate and event orders
- Rush-order and special-design fees
- Seasonal and holiday collections

### 6. Key Resources

- Baking skills and tested recipes
- Brand identity and design portfolio
- Licensed home or commercial kitchen
- Baking equipment, tools, and storage
- Reliable ingredient and packaging suppliers
- Website, social media accounts, and customer list
- Delivery vehicle or courier partner
- Product photography

### 7. Key Activities

- Developing and testing recipes
- Baking, decorating, and packaging
- Managing orders and customer requests
- Purchasing ingredients and controlling inventory
- Scheduling safe and timely deliveries
- Creating social media content
- Managing food safety, permits, and bookkeeping
- Collecting reviews and improving products

### 8. Key Partners

- Ingredient and baking-supply vendors
- Packaging and printing companies
- Local delivery drivers or courier services
- Florists, photographers, and gift shops
- Event planners and party decorators
- Cafes and local retailers
- Payment-processing and online-ordering providers

### 9. Cost Structure

- Ingredients and cake decorations
- Packaging, labels, and business cards
- Kitchen rent, utilities, permits, and insurance
- Baking equipment and maintenance
- Delivery fuel or courier charges
- Website, payment, and ordering fees
- Advertising and promotional samples
- Labor, photography, and bookkeeping

## Launch Priorities

- [ ] Choose a small menu of reliable bestsellers
- [ ] Define available customization options
- [ ] Set prices and custom-order deposits
- [ ] Define the delivery area and delivery fees
- [ ] Confirm local permits, insurance, and food-safety requirements
- [ ] Select packaging and suppliers
- [ ] Prepare product photos and social media profiles
- [ ] Set up an ordering and payment process
- [ ] Create launch promotions and a referral offer
