# Sweet Moments

## Project Overview

**Brand name:** Sweet Moment Cake  
**Business:** Handmade cake delivery  
**Stage:** Planning  
**Year:** 2026

> Fresh, handmade cakes that turn birthdays, celebrations, and thoughtful gifts into sweet moments through personalized designs and convenient local delivery.

## Business Goals

- Launch a trusted local handmade cake brand
- Offer fresh, attractive, and customizable cakes
- Make ordering and delivery simple and reliable
- Build repeat business through personal service
- Grow through referrals, social media, and local partnerships

## Launch Priorities

- [ ] Choose a small menu of reliable bestsellers
- [ ] Define cake sizes, flavors, and customization options
- [ ] Set prices, deposits, and cancellation terms
- [ ] Define the delivery area and delivery fees
- [ ] Confirm permits, insurance, and food-safety requirements
- [ ] Select ingredient and packaging suppliers
- [ ] Photograph products and prepare social media profiles
- [ ] Set up ordering and payment
- [ ] Plan launch promotions and a referral offer
